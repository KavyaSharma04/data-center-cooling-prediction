1. PROJECT ANALYSIS & ROOT CAUSES OF BASELINE LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Previous baseline models (LightGBM, Random Forest, XGBoost, Polynomial Regression)
struggled to achieve strong predictive performance on the test set:
- Baseline LightGBM:     MAE = 9.0779 kW | RMSE = 14.0363 kW | R² = -0.0860
- Polynomial Regression: MAE = 10.5365 kW | RMSE = 13.9797 kW | R² = -0.0772
- Baseline XGBoost:      MAE = 8.6324 kW | RMSE = 12.1141 kW | R² = 0.1911

Key discoveries from in-depth diagnostic analysis:
1. Severe Out-of-Distribution Shift in IT Load:
   - Training IT Power Mean: 1,082 kW (Max: 2,231 kW).
   - Test IT Power Mean:     2,527 kW (Max: 3,630 kW).
   - Over 89.26% of test samples exceed the maximum IT load ever seen in training.
   - Decision-tree ensembles cannot extrapolate linear trends beyond training boundaries,
     leading to severe under-prediction at high server workloads.
2. Correlation Inversion:
   - In 2016-2022, outdoor temperature had a negative correlation with cooling power (-0.30).
   - In 2023-2025, facility capacity expansion inverted the correlation to strongly positive (+0.60 to +0.75).
3. Missing Psychrometric & Thermodynamic Variables:
   - Evaporative cooling towers and chillers depend on Wet-Bulb temperature, Dew Point,
     and Moist Air Enthalpy rather than dry-bulb temperature alone.
4. Thermal Inertia & Facility Operational States:
   - Chilled water loops, thermal storage, and economizers exhibit multi-hour hysteresis.

2. IMPROVED METHODOLOGY & ARCHITECTURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We introduced two complementary high-performance modeling architectures implemented
in train_improved_model.py:

Architecture 1: Physics-Guided Cooling Ratio Model (Exogenous / Digital Twin Mode)
- Target: Cooling Load Ratio = cooling_kw / max(it_power_kw, 50.0).
- Scales seamlessly into out-of-distribution IT power regimes.
- Incorporates Stull wet-bulb formula, Magnus dew point, moist air enthalpy,
  cooling degree hours (CDH base 55F and 65F), free-cooling potential proxies,
  cyclic calendar harmonics (sin/cos of hour, DOW, DOY, month), and thermal lags.

Architecture 2: Dynamic Telemetry-Aware Autoregressive Model (Sequential Forecasting)
- Designed for real-time SCADA / BMS operation.
- Combines the full psychrometric feature space with short-term autoregressive
  chiller feedback (cooling_lag_1, cooling_lag_2, cooling_lag_3, cooling_lag_24,
  rolling standard deviation, and rolling averages).
